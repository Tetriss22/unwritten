AN ASCII ACTION GAME · ONE SMALL WORLD · FIVE LANDS
Something is deleting the world, one word at a time. You are the last Scribe. It is an open world: no walls, five lands, any order. Ride, climb the towers, light fires, find the letters and write the world back.
